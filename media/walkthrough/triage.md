## Triage from the sidebar

- **My Incidents** shows what's assigned to you. Triggered incidents show as a badge on the PagerDuty icon.
- **My Teams** and **All Incidents** use the same list, with search and filters in the view title bar.
- **On Call Now** lists who is level 1 on each escalation policy.

New incidents assigned to you pop up a notification with an **Acknowledge** button.
